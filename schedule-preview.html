The refuge

East Sixth Street, five minutes from Tompkins Square Park.

Caravan of Dreams has fed the East Village since 1991.

Its name echoes a Sufi teaching. The mystic Bahaudin Naqshband described humanity as a dream-caravan: individuals traveling together, yet each suspended in their own subjective reality, carried by their own hopes and illusions.

This week, we hope to gather those dreams together, right here in NYC.

As you enter the space, you’ll discover a bohemian restaurant with a small stage and a kitchen that runs all day: warm coffee, healthy snacks, exquisite plates, and drinks with or without alcohol. Behind it, the private Dream Room has a screen and A/V, for the conversations that want a door closed.

The doors are open from 11 AM to 11 PM, all week, for the curious locals and the weary travelers alike.

The curation

Our guidelines

We curated for the best possible mixture of humans. Every event on this schedule follows the same guidelines:

  • It cross-pollinates. It lives at the intersection of worlds that do not usually share a room.
  • It builds capacity. People leave with actions they can take.
  • It senses into the long arc of time. Forget quarterly profits and short-term false solutions.
  • It sells nothing. No pitches, no demos: this is not a corporate demo day.
Field trip

East Village Tour: Energy and Innovation

Wednesday, September 23 · 11 AM–12:30 PM · departs and ends at Caravan of Dreams

The city talks about the energy transition all week. This walk shows you where it is already happening.

Drop your bag at Caravan of Dreams and walk the Lower East Side with Wendy Brawer, the eco-designer and mapmaker who founded Green Map System here in 1995. Green Map is an open-source mapping language: a shared set of icons that communities around the world use to chart their own green sites, from solar roofs to community gardens to repair shops. Wendy’s newest is the LES Power Green Map-zine, and this tour visits its sites in person: the places where the neighborhood’s energy transition is taking root.

Along the way, Wendy shows how the maps get made and how to start a Green Map where you live. One morning of Climate Week spent at street level, instead of in a panel room. Come walk it with us.

You will be back by lunchtime; lunch afterward happens in Caravan’s public front of house.
