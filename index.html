The refuge

The back room. East Sixth Street, five minutes from Tompkins Square Park.

The Caravan has fed the East Village since 1991. For one week it becomes an urban refuge to nourish mind, body and spirit: a place to land between events, eat well, and find the people building what comes next.

The name echoes a Sufi teaching. The mystic Bahaudin Naqshband described humanity as a dream-caravan: travelers moving through time together, each carried by their own hopes. This week, the caravan gathers here.

Open from 11 AM – 11 PM, Caravan of Dreams will serve coffee, drinks, and food to the curious locals and weary travelers.
